# Curated Microduck wardrobe

24 looks across six collections. The previous catalog has been replaced completely.

Each complete look stores an independent item ID (or an empty slot) for hat, eyewear, clothes, accessories and legwear. Selecting a piece replaces only its category; removing a piece leaves the others intact. Part catalogs photograph the actual item on its own rather than showing the whole outfit again.

The design direction is a miniature designer toy: everyday clothing first, a few fantasy surprises, restrained palettes, curved silhouettes, rounded hems, layered collars, seams, closures and pockets. Clothing keeps the neck and its actuators clear. Hats and eyewear follow the head, torso garments follow the trunk, and paired shoes or warmers follow the corresponding native leg bodies.

Eyewear is designed around Microduck's single central eye: one lens and one closed frame, with style variations in round, clear, cat-eye, aviator, sport and tinted designs. Side temples attach the eyewear without adding a second frame.

The orange shell and accent palette are independent of clothing colors. Saved combinations and exports include both body colors and the five selected item IDs. Changing language preserves the same look and updates names, descriptions and controls.

The three representative silhouettes are Butter walk's cable cardigan, Rain check's layered cape, and Moon garden's layered petal skirt. Geometry is authored in metres using Microduck's original default-pose attachment frames; it is reproduced in URDF and MJCF as massless visual attachments. There is no neck ornament category.
